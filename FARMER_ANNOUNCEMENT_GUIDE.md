# How to Check Your Announcements

## For Farmers

### Where to Find Announcements

#### Option 1: Main Announcements Section
1. Log in to your Farmer Dashboard  
2. Click: **Farmer Dashboard** → **Announcements**
3. You'll see all announcements meant for:
   - Everyone (All Farmers), AND
   - Your farming group

#### Option 2: Weather Section
1. Log in to your Farmer Dashboard
2. Click: **Farmer Dashboard** → **Weather**
3. Here you'll see:
   - Weather alerts and updates
   - Important weather-related announcements
   - Storm warnings
   - Seasonal planting recommendations

---

## What Announcements Will You See?

You'll see announcements from your administrators for:
- ✅ Information about your farming group
- ✅ Weather and climate updates for your region
- ✅ Government programs and subsidies
- ✅ Training and educational notifications
- ✅ Emergency alerts
- ✅ Market price updates
- ✅ Event invitations

---

## Using Announcements

### Viewing an Announcement
1. Click on the announcement title to read full details
2. See the date it was posted
3. See the category (General, Alert, Weather)

### Searching Announcements
1. Use the **Search box** to find announcements
2. Search by keywords (e.g., "fertilizer", "rice", "weather")

### Filtering by Category
1. Use the **Category filter** dropdown
2. Choose:
   - **All** - See everything
   - **General** - Regular information
   - **Alert** - Important/urgent notices
   - **Weather** - Weather related

### Sorting
- Announcements show newest first
- Older announcements may be archived/hidden

## Privacy & Settings

### Who Can Send You Announcements?
Only your administrator and government officials can send announcements. You cannot opt-out of announcements meant for your farming group, but this ensures you don't miss important information.

### Why Different Announcements for Different Groups?
Because farmers in different regions have different needs:
- Different crops
- Different weather patterns  
- Different local programs
- Different local resources

### What If You Miss an Announcement?
- Announcements stay visible for a period of time
- Check regularly (at least weekly) for important updates
- Mark important dates in your calendar
- Tell your farming group leader if you miss something

---

## Tips for Farmers

1. **Check Regularly** - At least once a week
2. **Check Before Major Decisions** - Before planting, spraying, or harvesting
3. **Share Announcements** - Tell your group members about important news
4. **Act Promptly** - Some deadlines are time-sensitive (subsidies, applications, etc.)
5. **Ask Questions** - If unclear, contact your group leader or agricultural extension officer
